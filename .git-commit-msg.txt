Major upgrade to the audio processing pipeline:

- Two-pass EBU R128 loudness normalization: measures true integrated loudness
  first, then applies with measured values for broadcast-quality results
  (single-pass loudnorm was a rough approximation)
- RMS-based silence removal with padding: more natural than peak detection,
  preserves short pauses for natural speech flow, removes all silences not
  just leading silence
- Low-pass filter (16kHz): removes high-frequency hiss that speech doesn't need
- De-essing: reduces harsh sibilance (s/sh/ch) around 6kHz
- Speech EQ: gentle presence boost at 3kHz + warmth at 200Hz for intelligibility
- Soft limiter (alimiter): prevents digital clipping after normalization
- Configurable noise reduction strength (0-30) with adaptive FFT denoising
- Preserves mono source as mono (don't upsample to stereo unnecessarily)
- Higher default bitrate (160k, was 128k) with configurable quality
- Processing result metadata: original/final LUFS, size, duration
- Detailed logging with elapsed time and loudness measurements
- Admin settings UI: full control over all processing parameters including
  true peak, silence padding, de-essing, speech EQ, limiter, noise strength,
  and MP3 bitrate selection
