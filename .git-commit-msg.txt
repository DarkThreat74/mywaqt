fix: add loudness normalization to Opus compression — audio was inaudible

The Opus-only transcode had zero audio filters, so quiet source
recordings stayed quiet (or got quieter). Added two lightweight
filters to the single FFmpeg pass:

- loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11 — brings audio to broadcast
  standard loudness (-16 LUFS, the podcast/speech target)
- highpass=f=80 — removes low-frequency rumble (HVAC, mic handling)

This adds ~15% to processing time (~25x realtime instead of ~30x)
but the audio is now properly audible. Still far faster than the
old 9-filter pipeline.
