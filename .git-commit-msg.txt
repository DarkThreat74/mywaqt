fix: replace loudnorm with dynaudnorm to fix audio glitching/pumping

Research via agent-reach found that single-pass loudnorm is the
known cause of audio "glitching" on speech:

  "Single-pass loudnorm pumps; don't use it for VOD. In one pass,
  loudnorm doesn't know what's coming, so it makes loudness decisions
  on the fly with dynamic processing. On dialogue that breathes
  audibly."

Also: loudnorm internally upsamples to 192kHz which can cause
artifacts when encoding to Opus at low bitrates.

Fix:
- Replaced loudnorm with dynaudnorm (frame-by-frame gain with
  Gaussian smoothing — designed for speech, no pumping)
- Set maxgain=5 to avoid amplifying silence/noise excessively
- Pinned sample rate to 48000 with -ar 48000

Filter chain now:
  highpass(80) → lowpass(16k) → silenceremove(>2s, 1s buffer) →
  dynaudnorm(maxgain=5) → Opus 24kbps 48kHz
