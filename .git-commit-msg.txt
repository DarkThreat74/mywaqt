feat: collapsible folders in admin talks view

Folders now have a chevron toggle to collapse/expand. Click the
folder name or chevron to toggle. Collapsed folders hide their
talk list but remain visible in the sidebar with their talk count.

This is essential for managing large libraries — a folder with
300 talks no longer pushes other folders off screen.

The Uncategorized section is also collapsible.
