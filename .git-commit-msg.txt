fix: revert to ffmpeg-static, remove leave_silence option causing filter errors

@ffmpeg-installer/ffmpeg ships FFmpeg 4.1 from 2018 — way too old.
Reverted to ffmpeg-static (FFmpeg 6.1.1).

Root cause of "Option not found" filter errors: the leave_silence
option in silenceremove is not available in all ffmpeg-static builds.
Removed it and simplified silenceremove:

Before (failed):
  silenceremove=start_periods=1:start_duration=0.5:...:leave_silence=1

After (works):
  silenceremove=stop_periods=-1:stop_duration=2:stop_threshold=-50dB

This removes all silence longer than 2 seconds entirely. Pauses
under 2 seconds are preserved. The 1-second replacement pause is
dropped since leave_silence isn't reliably available.

Full chain: highpass(80) → lowpass(16k) → silenceremove(>2s) → loudnorm(-16 LUFS) → Opus
