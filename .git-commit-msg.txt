Added silenceremove filter to the Opus compression pipeline:
- Trims leading silence (0.5s+ at start)
- Caps all mid-file pauses at 1 second
- Pauses under 1 second are preserved as-is
- Uses leave_silence=1 so 1 second of silence is kept at each
  cut point (natural pacing between sentences)

Filter chain is now: highpass → silenceremove → loudnorm → Opus encode
